Store Policies, Warranty & Trade-In Guidelines

Shop #1, Silverest Complex, Great East Road, Lusaka (Opposite UNILUS)

1. 12-Month Official Warranty

Every brand-new hardware device sold by MacMo Electronics includes an official 12-Month warranty against manufacturer defects. Certified pre-owned Grade A+ devices come with a 90-day comprehensive hardware warranty.

  • Covered: Motherboard defects, internal component failure, factory display faults, factory camera failures.
  • Not Covered: Physical accidental damage (cracked screens, drop impact), water immersion beyond IP rating, unauthorized third-party repairs, software rooting/jailbreaks.

2. Device Trade-In / Swap Inspection Criteria

Our Trade-In Valuation Calculator gives an instant estimate. The final valuation is confirmed upon physical testing at Shop #1 Silverest Complex:

  • Flawless (100% Value): Zero scratches, original screen, battery health 85%+, all sensors functional (FaceID/Fingerprint), iCloud/Google account removed.
  • Good (85% Value): Minor microscopic micro-scratches from normal case usage, 100% functional display and camera, original components.
  • Fair (65% Value): Noticeable cosmetic scratches or minor scuffs on frame, clean glass with no structural cracks, fully functional motherboard.

3. Lusaka Delivery & Campus Perks

We provide FREE same-day delivery to University of Lusaka (UNILUS) Silverest Campus hostels and faculty offices. For the greater Lusaka metropolitan area, rapid motorbike/courier dispatch operates Monday to Saturday. Countrywide deliveries to Kitwe, Ndola, Livingstone, and Solwezi are serviced via EMS or Power Tools bus courier with tracking.

4. Privacy & Customer Data Protection

MacMo Electronics respects your privacy. Phone numbers and order details collected for WhatsApp communication and invoices are strictly used for delivery fulfillment, warranty tracking, and customer support. We never share or sell customer data to third parties.
