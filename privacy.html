Warranty, Returns & Store Policies

Clear, transparent rules governing hardware warranties, 7-day replacements, 0% Layby price locks, trade-in inspections, countrywide courier shipping, and corporate procurement at MacMo Electronics Lusaka (Town Center & Silverest).

1. 12-Month Official Warranty & Coverage

Every brand-new hardware device purchased from MacMo Electronics includes an official 12-Month comprehensive manufacturer hardware warranty. Certified pre-owned Grade A+ devices include a 90-day store hardware warranty.

  • What is Covered: Motherboard electronic defects, spontaneous component failure, factory display panel faults (lines or touch failure not resulting from drops), camera sensor defects, and internal charging circuitry.
  • What is Not Covered: Accidental physical impact (cracked screens, bent chassis, dented corners), liquid submersion beyond official ingress specs, power surge damage from uncertified adapters, unauthorized 3rd-party repairs, or jailbroken/rooted firmware.
  • Claim Procedure: Present your digital or printed receipt at either of our Lusaka branches: Town Center (Voltic Building, Kalundwe Rd & Cha-Cha Rd Corner) or Silverest (Asado Complex Shop #1, Opposite UNILUS Great East Road). Customers outside Lusaka (Copperbelt, Livingstone, Solwezi) may dispatch their device to our Lusaka workshop via EMS Courier with real-time tracking.
Turnaround Promise: Warranty diagnostic assessments are completed within 48 to 72 hours of store receipt.

2. 7-Day Immediate Replacement & Returns Policy

We hold every unit to rigorous Quality Control. If an item exhibits a technical or factory hardware fault right out of the box, we protect you with an immediate 7-day exchange window:

  • Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) / Out-of-Box Fault: Devices exhibiting manufacturer faults within 7 days of purchase qualify for an immediate 1-to-1 brand-new replacement or 100% store credit.
  • Change of Mind: Sealed brand-new devices in intact factory plastic wrap may be returned within 48 hours for an exchange or store voucher. Once unsealed or activated under an Apple ID / Google account, change-of-mind returns are not accepted.
  • Return Requirements: The unit must be accompanied by its original retail box, supplied charging accessories, and proof of purchase (MacMo receipt or proforma reference).
Inspection Check: Serial numbers and IMEIs on returned hardware must match the recorded store invoice.

3. 0% Interest Layby (Layaway) Agreement & Terms

MacMo’s 0% Layby program allows students, professionals, and families to secure premium tech with zero debt and zero interest:

  • Deposit Requirements: Pay an initial deposit of 20%, 30%, or 50% to immediately reserve the device.
  • Price Lock Guarantee: The Kwacha (ZMW) price agreed upon at the time of your deposit is 100% locked. Even if market prices or USD exchange rates rise, your price will never increase.
  • Payoff Tenure: Pay off the balance at your own pace over 1 to 3 calendar months via Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, bank transfer, or cash.
  • Vault Storage: Your specific device is cataloged, labelled, and held securely in our Town Center or Silverest vault until completion.
  • Grace Period & Cancellations: A 14-day grace period is granted upon notifying our WhatsApp team. If a customer chooses to cancel a layby plan, all accumulated payments are converted into a 100% Store Credit Voucher (valid for 12 months for any tech purchase), or refunded minus a standard 10% administrative restocking fee.
Zero Hidden Fees: No interest, no monthly penalties, and zero administrative surcharges.

4. Device Trade-In, Swap & Anti-Theft Guidelines

Upgrade your smartphone, laptop, or PlayStation console by trading in your existing tech:

  • Condition Valuation Tiers:
    • Flawless (100% Value): Zero scratches, original display, battery health 85%+, all sensors functional (FaceID/Fingerprint), iCloud/Google account removed.
    • Good (85% Value): Microscopic wear from normal case usage, flawless display and camera, original factory components.
    • Fair (65% Value): Cosmetic frame scuffs, clean structural glass, 100% functional motherboard.
  • Strict Anti-Theft / Clean Ownership: MacMo strictly enforces clean device verification. All trade-in serial numbers and IMEIs are cross-referenced. Stolen, blacklisted, or MDM-supervised hardware is strictly rejected and reported.
  • Customer Data & Certified Wipe: Customers must back up personal photos and sign out of Apple ID / Google accounts before handover. MacMo performs a secondary certified cryptographic data wipe on every accepted unit.

5. Payment Methods, MoMo Verification & Anti-Fraud

We support fast, safe payments through Zambia's leading financial rails:

  • Accepted Payment Methods: Airtel Money Merchant, MTN MoMo, Absa Bank ZMW electronic funds transfer (EFT), and Cash (in-store or on delivery).
  • Merchant Verification: To protect both customers and the store against SMS spoofing scams, goods are dispatched only once funds reflect in MacMo’s corporate merchant terminal or bank account.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Limits: Available within the greater Lusaka metropolitan area for verified phone orders up to K 10,000. Orders exceeding K 10,000 require advance MoMo/bank settlement or in-store collection for driver safety.
  • Zero Transaction Surcharges: Prices displayed on the website are final—no hidden card or checkout fees.
