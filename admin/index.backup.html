How to Connect Your Firebase Login Account

When you create your account on Google Firebase:
1. Go to console.firebase.google.com and create a project.
2. Enable Authentication (Email/Password provider) and add your admin email & password.
3. In Firestore Database, click Create Database (start in Test mode).
4. Open firebase-config.js in this folder, paste your keys, and change enabled: false to enabled: true.
Once saved, this portal automatically switches to full Google Cloud Authentication!
